# AI Impact Survey for SaaS Product Managers  

## Methodology Note (One‑Page)  

**Purpose**  
Measure how SaaS product managers perceive the impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on their product development workflows, decision‑making, team dynamics, and business outcomes.  

**Target Population**  
SaaS product managers (PMs) employed at B2B or B2C software companies with ≥10 employees. No geographic restrictions.  

**Sampling**  
Convenience sampling via product‑management communities (Slack, LinkedIn groups, newsletters) and direct outreach to SaaS founders. Minimum viable N = 150 for basic descriptive analysis; 300+ enables subgroup splits (e.g., by company size, AI maturity).  

**Data Collection**  
- Hosted on Google Forms (link distributed via email or community posts).  
- Anonymous; no personally identifiable information collected.  
- Estimated completion time: 8‑10 minutes.  

**Ethical Considerations**  
Participation is voluntary. Consent is implied by submission. Data stored in Google Drive with access limited to the researcher. No compensation offered.  

**Limitations**  
Self‑selection bias may over‑represent PMs already interested in AI. Cross‑sectional design precludes causal inference.  

**Analysis Overview**  
Descriptive statistics (frequencies, means, medians) for all items. Bivariate analyses (Chi‑square, t‑tests, ANOVA) to test differences by AI adoption level, company size, and PM tenure. Open‑ended responses coded inductively into themes.
