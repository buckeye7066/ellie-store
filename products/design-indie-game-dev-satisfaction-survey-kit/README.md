## Methodology Note (one‑page)

**Purpose**  
Measure satisfaction, pain points, and motivations of indie game developers across the full development lifecycle to inform product improvements, community support, and marketing strategies.

**Target Population**  
Individuals who identify as indie game developers (solo or small team ≤10 people) who have worked on at least one game project in the past 12 months, regardless of release status.

**Sampling**  
Convenience sample recruited via indie dev forums (IndieDB, TIGSource, r/gamedev), Discord communities, and newsletter lists. No quotas; open to all who click the link.

**Data Collection**  
Self‑administered online questionnaire hosted on Google Forms. Estimated completion time: 8‑10 minutes. Anonymous; no personal identifiers collected unless voluntarily supplied in open‑ended field.

**Ethics**  
Participation is voluntary; respondents may skip any question or withdraw at any time by closing the form. Data stored in Google Drive with access limited to the purchaser. No IP tracking enabled.

**Limitations**  
Self‑selection bias may over‑represent highly engaged developers. Cross‑sectional design precludes causal inference. Language limited to English.

**Data Handling**  
Export responses as CSV from Google Forms. Clean open‑ended responses using the coding sheet provided. Perform descriptive analysis per the analysis plan; optionally run reliability checks (Cronbach’s α) on multi‑item scales.
