## Methodology Note (One‑Page)

**Purpose**  
Assess the extent to which a health‑tech startup’s AI‑driven products and processes meet core ethical principles: privacy, fairness, transparency, accountability, safety, and regulatory compliance. The checklist yields a quantitative compliance score and identifies priority improvement areas.

**Target Respondents**  
Founders, CEOs, CTOs, product leads, data scientists, and compliance officers directly involved in AI model development, deployment, or oversight.

**Administration**  
- Self‑administered online survey (Qualtrics, Google Forms, or similar).  
- Estimated completion time: 12‑15 minutes.  
- Anonymous; identifier collected only for follow‑up if consented.

**Scoring**  
Each item is coded numerically (see Response‑Coding Sheet). Domain scores are the mean of items within that domain (excluding “Not Applicable”). Overall compliance score = weighted average of domain scores (weights: Privacy 20%, Fairness 20%, Transparency 15%, Accountability 15%, Safety 15%, Regulatory 15%). Scores range 1‑5; ≥4 = “Strong Compliance”, 3‑3.99 = “Moderate”, <3 = “Weak”.

**Interpretation**  
- Highlight domains with mean <3.5 for immediate action.  
- Use open‑ended feedback (optional) to contextualize low scores.  
- Track score changes over time (baseline, 6‑month, annual) to monitor progress.

**Limitations**  
Self‑report bias; does not replace formal audit or legal counsel. Intended as a diagnostic tool for internal improvement and investor communication.
