## 1. Introduction  
Small businesses often lack the budget for costly SaaS AI services, yet they can benefit enormously from automating repetitive text‑based tasks. Running a large language model (LLM) locally eliminates per‑token fees, gives full data control, and works offline—critical for businesses handling sensitive customer or financial information.  

This guide walks you through setting up a local LLM on a modest workstation or laptop, selecting the right model, and building five ready‑to‑use automation pipelines. Each workflow includes exact commands, prompt templates, and sample outputs you can copy‑paste into your own scripts or no‑code tools (e.g., Zapier via webhooks, Make, or simple batch files).
