## Overview
Freelancers often rewrite the same invoice script, only to have it break when tax rates change or a new client is added. This Notion template eliminates that chore by providing a single source of truth for clients, line‑items, and invoices, with built‑in formulas that automatically compute subtotal, tax, and total amounts.  

All data lives in Notion databases; no external code, no scripts to maintain. The template works on the free Notion plan and can be exported to PDF or printed directly from Notion.

---  

## Features
- **Client Database** – store contact info, billing address, default tax rate, and notes.  
- **Invoice Database** – each invoice links to a client, contains a roll‑up of line‑items, and calculates subtotal, tax, and total automatically.  
- **Line‑Item Database** (optional) – reusable services/products with description, unit price, and quantity; can be added ad‑hoc per invoice.  
- **Automatic Tax Calculation** – tax rate pulled from the client record (overrideable per invoice).  
- **Status Tracking** – draft, sent, paid, overdue with colour‑coded tags.  
- **Payment Due Date** – auto‑highlights overdue invoices.  
- **PDF‑Ready View** – a clean, printable invoice layout using Notion’s built‑in page styling.  
- **License** – MIT‑licensed, free to modify and resell as part of your own product.
