## Executive Summary  
Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retriever that fetches relevant passages from a knowledge base with a generator (usually a large language model) that conditions on those passages to produce answers. For domain‑specific chatbots—where accuracy, up‑to‑date information, and adherence to regulated terminology are critical—RAG offers a pragmatic alternative to full model fine‑tuning.
