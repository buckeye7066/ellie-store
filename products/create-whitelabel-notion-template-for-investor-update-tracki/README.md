## 2. Overview  

This Notion template enables early‑stage founders to capture, track, and communicate investor updates in a single, linked workspace. It consists of three core databases:  

1. **Cap Table** – equity ownership, share classes, and dilution scenarios.  
2. **Updates** – timestamped investor communications (quarterly updates, ad‑hoc notes, KPI snapshots).  
3. **Investor Contacts** – master list of investors with communication preferences and interaction history.  

All databases are linked via relations and roll‑ups so that a single update can automatically reflect the latest cap‑table numbers, and each investor view shows a chronological history of updates sent to them.  

The template is deliberately unbranded: placeholder text (`[Your Company Name]`, `[Logo]`, etc.) appears where you would insert your own branding. All views, formulas, and automations are generic and ready for immediate re‑skin.
